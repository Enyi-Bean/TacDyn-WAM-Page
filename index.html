The core idea

Predict the dynamics of touch,
not its pixels.

Tactile pixels can change sharply under small contact shifts. TacDyn-WAM forecasts compact, dynamics-aware tactile representations alongside visual futures, then uses both to predict actions.

Learning TacRep

A representation built for contact evolution.

Masked tactile clips teach the encoder predictable temporal change. A frozen DINOv2 teacher regularizes local patch relations without forcing tactile features into a static image space.
